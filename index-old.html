About the Festival

Why Canberra Data Week?

Canberra is Australia's data capital, home to the ABS, federal policy institutions and world-class research universities. Data Week is a free, open, citywide festival that puts that expertise to work for the whole community.

🏙️

Distributed & Citywide

A central anchor at Microsoft's Canberra office, plus satellite events hosted by partner organisations across offices, museums, galleries, lakeside and online.

🤝

Open to Everyone

All events are free to attend. Whether you're a data scientist, a public servant, a student or just curious, there is a Canberra Data Week event for you.

💡

Community-Driven

Any organisation can apply to host an event. We invite teams and individuals to design and run their own session as an official part of the week's program.

Our Pillar

Data for Public Good

Everything at Canberra Data Week connects back to one goal: using data to deliver better outcomes for people.

🏥

Better Services

Improving how government and community services reach the people who need them most.

📊

Evidence-Led Policy

Supporting responsive, effective policy through rigorous and timely data.

🔍

Transparency

Open data, open government and accountability to the communities data affects.

🌏

Citizen Outcomes

Putting data in the service of real people, and giving communities a voice in how it is used.

Host an Event

How to Get Involved

Hosting a Canberra Data Week event is open to any organisation, team or individual with something valuable to share. Here is the pathway from idea to event.

1

Explore the Event Guide

Review the event types, format options, audience guidance and the Data for Public Good pillar to see where your idea fits in the program.

2

Register Your Interest

Submit a brief expression of interest online, covering who you are, your event concept and your preferred format and venue type.

3

Attend an Information Webinar

Join one of our free online briefings to learn more about hosting, ask questions and refine your event concept before applying.

4

Submit Your Application

Complete the formal application with your event concept, target audience, format, accessibility plan and preferred venue, whether Microsoft Canberra, the National Museum, a cultural arts venue or a public outdoor space.

5

Receive Confirmation & Onboarding

Approved events are listed on the public program. You receive branding assets, planning support and promotion through Canberra Data Week channels.

6

Host Your Event

Deliver your event during Canberra Data Week. You own the content and experience; we provide the platform, promotion and coordination.

What We Look For

  • Relevance to data, evidence, analytics or data literacy
  • Alignment with the Data for Public Good pillar
  • Free and open attendance for all participants
  • A clear audience and achievable event format
  • Inclusion and accessibility considered in the design
  • A nominated lead contact from your organisation
  • A confirmed or proposed venue for in-person events
